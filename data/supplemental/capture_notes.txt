Supplemental source capture notes

The published repository includes D074.txt, the official San Diego Municipal
Code Chapter 9, Article 8, Division 11. D074_capture_log.json records the original
manifest URL, the official replacement PDF URL, retrieval-time basis, PDF hash,
text hash and extraction method. The organizer pack remains unchanged.

The local workspace additionally retains five earlier research captures from
the supplied manifest URLs: D035, D037, D059, D086 and D087. These third-party
full-text files and their historical fetch_log.json are not published here.
SOURCE records the captured source URL. RETRIEVED identifies capture time, not
the law's effective date or the competition's 2026-10-01 query date.

The current rules reference D037 (Morgan Lewis), D059 (WBUR), and D074 among
supplemental sources. Full provenance validation needs the original D037/D059
snapshots as well as the published originals and D074. The shipped validation
report records a check in the full local workspace. A clean clone can validate
the official JSON schema but cannot reproduce every source-text check without
those two private snapshots. Missing-source errors must not be suppressed.

Run python3 scripts/fetch_sources.py --dry-run to inspect the five reviewed
sources. Run without --dry-run to retrieve new copies. Use --documents to select
one or more of D035, D037, D059, D086 and D087. New captures can differ from the
October 3, 2026 copies because live pages, navigation and parser formatting change.
The script preserves source text and copyright footers, while mechanically
omitting script, style, head, SVG and noscript elements. It does not summarize or
manually supply missing legal text. A failed refresh retains any earlier local
capture and records the failure and previous capture in the log.

Local capture is not a grant of permission to republish article text. Keep the
five third-party full-text copies and model full-text caches out of public repositories. Any public
submission should separately assess its use of short quotations and source links.
Morgan Lewis permits personal, noncommercial downloads with copyright notices
retained; this is not permission to redistribute the full article.

The script refuses eCode360 sources because the reviewed terms restrict copying
for public/non-profit purposes and derivative works. It refuses GoCodebook because
section 2.2(d) prohibits automated access outside documented APIs. It also refuses
all other sources beyond the five reviewed URLs. TLS validation is always enabled;
there are no authenticated requests, CAPTCHA workarounds, or retries that bypass
HTTP 403 responses. The tool performs individual requests, not a site crawl.

The five earlier captures are secondary reporting; D074 is official code text.
D037 covers multiple jurisdictions despite the manifest's Jersey City label and
contains a Berkeley effective-date conflict with supplied official ordinance D001.
D087 reports the first reading of Santa Ana's ordinance; D086 later reports final
passage. Do not infer final enactment from a first-reading report. Monthly dates
in D037 must not be silently converted into precise days without further evidence. Recent-news navigation
may contain dates later than the competition query date and is not legal evidence.

The current status_verification records provide later official corroboration
and date findings where available. They do not turn every statement in an older
secondary source into a verified primary-law statement. D074 was acquired from
the official PDF and extracted with PDFKit; the five-URL fetch script does not
reconstruct D074. Re-downloading a live source may change text, dates and offsets.
